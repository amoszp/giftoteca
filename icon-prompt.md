# Prompt para el icono de Giftoteca

Pega el bloque de abajo (en inglés, para mejor resultado en la mayoría de generadores) en tu herramienta de imágenes.

---

Premium minimalist app icon design, flat vector illustration style, for a gift-tracking mobile app called "Giftoteca". Square 1:1 composition, designed to sit inside an iOS rounded-square (squircle) icon mask, generous padding, centered composition, no text, no watermark, no logo type.

Solid flat background color #741A2F (deep burgundy), no gradients, no texture, no noise.

Subject: a calico cat ("gato carei"), shown from the chest up, peeking over the top edge of a simple minimally-wrapped gift box. The cat's coat is rendered as clean tricolor patches — deep burgundy #741A2F, light salmon #FFC6A8, and a soft cream/off-white — in a calico patching pattern, simplified into elegant flat shapes rather than realistic fur texture. Warm, friendly, curious expression, simple dot or almond eyes, no cartoonish exaggeration, no big anime eyes.

The gift box sits at the bottom of the composition, wrapped with a single thin ribbon accent in light salmon #FFC6A8, flat color, no bow clutter, no gradients, no drop shadows.

Style reference: modern premium App Store icon design (think Apple Human Interface Guidelines quality), clean geometric shapes, confident negative space, limited flat color palette, single light source at most implied through shape not shading, vector illustration, crisp edges, professional icon designer quality — not a cute cartoon sticker, not clipart, not a children's book illustration, not 3D render.

Negative prompt / avoid: photorealism, fur texture detail, busy background, extra objects, multiple cats, text, watermark, signature, drop shadow, bevel, glossy plastic look, gradient background, more than three colors in the cat's coat.

---

## Notas

- El fondo va siempre en burdeos plano, el mismo color de acento que ya usa toda la aplicación.
- Las manchas del carei se piden solo en los tres tonos de marca (burdeos, salmón y un crema suave) para que el icono no rompa la paleta del resto de la app.
- Si el resultado sale demasiado ilustrado o infantil, añade al prompt: "flatter, more geometric, less illustrative, closer to a modern minimal app icon".
- Genera preferiblemente en formato cuadrado, mínimo 1024x1024, para poder recortarlo luego a los tamaños que pide el manifest de la aplicación (192x192, 512x512 y el icono de Apple).
